Privacy Policy (template)

This is a template. The clinic and its legal adviser must review and complete it before launch.

What this demo collects

The appointment form collects your name, mobile number, treatment, preferred date and time, and an optional message. It does not send them to any server. It builds a WhatsApp message on your device, and you choose whether to send it.

What is stored

Only your language choice is saved in your browser. Appointment details are not stored by this website. If you tap Show live map, Google Maps loads and Google may collect data under its own privacy policy.
